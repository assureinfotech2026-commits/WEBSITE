MODA

Laboratory Quality Management Software

MODA is a Laboratory Quality Management Software platform that streamlines deviations, CAPA, change control, complaints, audits, and document management in a single, audit-trailed environment. We help you implement, configure, validate, and support MODA to strengthen quality processes and accelerate regulatory compliance.

WHERE IT'S USED

Where It's Used

MODA supports a wide range of quality management scenarios across regulated industries.

Environmental Monitoring (EM)

Tracks viable and nonviable contamination across cleanrooms and critical areas, covering air, surface, personnel, and compressed gas sampling. • Utility and Product Testing: Captures and manages routine utility test data and product testing records within a unified digital workflow.

Field Data Capture (MODA-FDC)

Equips technicians with mobile tools—such as stainless steel cleanroom carts, tablet PCs, barcode scanners, thermal label printers, and RF badge readers—to collect and barcode samples directly at the point of sampling.

Instrument and System Integration

Connects directly with lab and manufacturing equipment—including particle counters, total organic carbon (TOC) meters, and endotoxin devices—as well as enterprise systems like LIMS, ERP, and SAP.

Real-Time Tracking, Trending, and Compliance

Provides location-based scheduling, automatic action/alert notifications, supervisory dashboards, and visual analytics (via MODA-VIP) to support data integrity, SOP compliance, and faster investigations.
