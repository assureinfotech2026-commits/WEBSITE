Important: We collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose, service delivery, legal compliance, or other lawful purposes.

We may collect information through the following methods:

We process personal information only where there is an appropriate lawful basis under applicable law.

We do not sell personal information to third parties as part of our ordinary business operations.

Information may be shared with:

  • Government Authorities: Relevant government departments, regulatory authorities, and statutory bodies where required for filings, registrations, verification, or legal compliance.
  • Payment Service Providers: Payment gateways, banks, and financial institutions for processing transactions and verifying payments.
  • Technology Service Providers: Website hosting providers, cloud storage providers, software providers, and IT infrastructure partners.
  • Professional Service Providers: Chartered accountants, company secretaries, advocates, consultants, and other authorised professionals where required for service delivery.
