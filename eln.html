ELN

Electronic Laboratory Notebook

An Electronic Laboratory Notebook (ELN) digitizes experiment design, execution, and review, replacing paper notebooks with structured, searchable, and audit-trailed electronic records. We help you implement ELN platforms that protect intellectual property, improve reproducibility, and accelerate scientific collaboration.

What We Offer

Comprehensive ELN services from initial strategy through long-term operational support.

ELN Implementation

Full lifecycle implementation of ELN platforms including requirement analysis, template design, configuration, UAT, validation, training, and go-live. We tailor the ELN to your scientific workflows rather than imposing rigid structures.

Experiment Template Design

Design of structured experiment templates that capture inputs, methods, observations, results, and conclusions in a consistent, searchable format. Templates improve reproducibility, simplify review, and accelerate scientific collaboration.

ELN Validation

Risk-based CSV validation of ELN platforms in line with GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11. We deliver complete validation documentation including IQ, OQ, PQ, traceability matrix, and validation summary report.

ELN Integration

Integration of ELN with CDS. We enable seamless data flow between systems, eliminating transcription and improving data integrity across the laboratory.

ELN Operational Support

Ongoing production support, template enhancement, user administration, and continuous improvement. We keep your ELN aligned with evolving scientific workflows and regulatory expectations.

VALUE

Key Benefits

A well-implemented ELN transforms how science is documented, shared, and protected.

Searchable Science

Full-text search across experiments, methods, and results makes historical knowledge instantly accessible to the entire team.

IP Protection

Audit-trailed, electronically signed records provide defensible intellectual property protection with clear attribution and timestamps.

Reproducibility

Structured templates and integrated data capture improve experimental reproducibility and reduce ambiguity in scientific records.

Collaboration

Electronic sharing, review workflows, and integrated commenting accelerate scientific collaboration across teams and sites.
