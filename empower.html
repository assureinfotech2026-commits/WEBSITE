Empower

Chromatography Data System

Waters Empower is one of the most widely deployed Chromatography Data Systems (CDS) in regulated laboratories. We help you implement, configure, validate, integrate, and support Empower environments that deliver defensible chromatographic data, streamlined review, and seamless connectivity with LIMS and enterprise systems.

What We Offer

End-to-end Empower services covering implementation, validation, integration, and operational support.

Empower Implementation

Implementation support of Empower including system configuration, validation, training, and go-live. We deliver Empower environments that are scientifically effective and regulatorily compliant.

Empower Validation

Risk-based CSV validation of Empower in line with GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11. We deliver IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols covering acquisition, processing, reporting, audit trail, and electronic signature functionality.

Empower Integration

Integration of Empower with LIMS for sample synchronization, result transfer, and audit trail alignment. We ensure chromatographic results flow automatically into the LIMS workflow for review, approval, and release.

Empower Operational Support

Tiered production support, user administration, change management, and continuous improvement. We keep your Empower environment healthy, compliant, and aligned with evolving laboratory and regulatory requirements.

Key Benefits

A well-managed Empower environment delivers scientific, operational, and regulatory benefits.

Data Integrity

Built-in audit trails, electronic signatures, and access controls ensure chromatographic data is attributable, traceable, and inspection-ready.

Faster Review Cycles

Automated result transfer to LIMS and structured review workflows reduce manual handoffs and accelerate sample release.

Regulatory Compliance

Validated Empower deployments satisfy 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, and global GMP expectations for chromatographic data.

Scalable Architecture

Properly designed Empower architectures support multi-instrument, multi-site deployments that scale with your laboratory's growth.

WHERE IT'S USED

Where It's Used

Empower supports a wide range of chromatographic applications in regulated laboratories.

Pharmaceutical QC

Assay, impurity, dissolution, and related substances testing for raw materials, in-process samples, and finished products.

Biotechnology

Protein characterization, peptide mapping, and aggregation analysis for biologics development and release.

Method Development

Systematic method development and validation with structured capture of conditions, results, and method performance metrics.

Food & Beverage

Pesticide residue, additive, contaminant, and nutritional analysis for food safety and quality control.

Chemical & Petrochemical

Compositional analysis, purity determination, and contamination testing for chemical and petrochemical products.

Stability Testing

Long-term stability programmes with chromatographic monitoring of potency, impurities, and degradation products over time.
