Interfaces

Modern laboratories run on a portfolio of specialized systems. Our interface services connect LIMS, ERP, CDS, MES, QMS, and instruments into a unified, audit-trailed ecosystem that eliminates silos, manual data transfer, and transcription errors.

What We Offer

A focused portfolio of interface services covering the most critical integration points in modern regulated laboratories.

LIMS Interface With ERP/HANA, Trackwise, MES, and Documentum

Design and deployment of bidirectional interfaces between LIMS and enterprise systems including ERP, SAP HANA, Trackwise QMS, MES, and Documentum. We ensure material, sample, result, specification, and document data flows securely with full audit traceability across the ecosystem.

Instrument Interface (Port & File Based and LabX)

Connection of analytical instruments to LIMS or ELN using port-based serial communication or file-based parsing mechanisms. We support a wide range of balances, pH meters, titrators, and other bench-top instruments, eliminating manual transcription and improving result integrity.

CDS Interface (Empower & Chromeleon)

Integration of chromatography data systems including Waters Empower and Thermo Chromeleon with LIMS for sample synchronization, result transfer, and audit trail alignment. We ensure CDS results are automatically routed, reviewed, and approved within the laboratory workflow.
